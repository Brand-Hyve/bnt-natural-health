Nutritional Health Coaching

Nutritional Health Coaching is a personalized, educational approach designed to support overall wellness through nutrition and lifestyle guidance. As a Nutritional Therapy Practitioner (NTP), Trish Dillon works with clients to better understand how everyday nutrition and lifestyle choices may influence the body’s natural balance and foundational wellness.

This client-centered service meets you where you are whether you are just beginning to explore healthier habits or looking to build consistency and confidence in your current routine. Rather than offering one-size-fits-all recommendations, Nutritional Health Coaching focuses on individualized education, realistic strategies, and sustainable habits that can be applied in daily life.

During Nutritional Health Coaching, we explore key wellness foundations such as food quality, dietary variety, hydration, lifestyle balance, and nutritional support. Education is provided in a supportive and empowering way, helping clients develop awareness of how daily choices may impact overall well-being over time.

Nutritional Health Coaching is educational in nature and is intended to support general wellness. As an NTP, Trish does not diagnose, treat, or cure medical conditions. Nutrition Response Testing® at B&T Natural Health & Wellness Association

This approach uses gentle muscle response testing as a way to observe how the body may be responding to various nutritional inputs. The information gathered helps guide personalized nutrition and lifestyle support intended to promote general wellness.

NRT does not diagnose or treat medical conditions. Instead, it is an educational tool that supports informed decision-making around nutrition and daily habits. Many individuals are drawn to Nutrition Response Testing® because it offers a personalized, holistic perspective that looks at the whole person rather than focusing on isolated symptoms.

During a Nutrition Response Testing® session, gentle muscle response testing is used to help assess patterns related to nutrition and lifestyle stress. This process is non-invasive and does not involve needles, machines, or medical procedures. The results are used to help guide individualized nutrition and lifestyle recommendations tailored to your unique needs and goals.
