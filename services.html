Nutrition Response Testing® (NRT)

A non-invasive, educational assessment method used to gather information about how the body may be responding to nutritional and lifestyle stressors.

Using gentle muscle response testing, this approach helps observe patterns that may suggest areas where supportive nutrition and lifestyle guidance may be helpful. The insights gathered help guide personalized recommendations designed to support overall wellness.

Neurological reflex

Neuro-Matrix Technique® (NMT)

A gentle, non-invasive approach designed to help identify patterns in how the body responds to physical stress, tension, and movement.

The technique focuses on reflex pathways within the nervous system — the communication between the brain, nerves, and muscles that influence posture, movement, and protective tension responses throughout the body.
