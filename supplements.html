Whole-Food Supplementation

Nutritional support personalized to your body's stress indicators and sourced from concentrated, organic foods.

Targeted, Food-Based Support

Many wellness programs rely on general supplement recommendations. At B&T Natural Health, we take a different approach. Since your body is unique, the supplement guidance we provide is personalized to support the specific stress patterns observed during your Nutrition Response Testing® assessments.

We specialize in whole-food supplementation from premium brands like Standard Process, MediHerb, and others. Unlike synthetic vitamins created in a lab, whole-food supplements are derived from concentrated organic foods, preserving the complex synergistic vitamins, minerals, and enzymes that your body is designed to recognize and absorb.

Why Whole Foods Matter

Synthetically manufactured vitamins contain isolated fractions of nutrients. Whole-food supplements represent the full spectrum of food compounds, meaning they are:

  • ✔ Concentrated Whole Foods — Made from organic vegetables, plants, and natural ingredients.
  • ✔ Highly Absorbable — Preserves natural cofactors that aid nutrient assimilation.
  • ✔ Targeted Support — Formulated to address specific nutritional imbalances identified by NRT.
  • ✔ Quality Tested — Free from synthetic fillers, artificial binders, and poor quality inputs.

Individualized Guidelines

Rather than taking a collection of random supplements, we guide you to only what your body shows a stress response to. We teach you what to take, why your body needs it, and how to monitor your progress. This targeted approach supports efficiency and clarity.

Important Note: Whole-food supplement guidance is educational in nature and intended to support general wellness through nutrition. We do not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or cure medical conditions. This approach is intended to complement medical care, not replace it. Clients are encouraged to consult with their licensed healthcare providers for medical decision-making.
